# ApnaTask brand foundation

Status: selected direction, production foundation v1.

## Brand idea

ApnaTask brings two people together around one protected task: a customer who
needs help and a verified provider who can do the work. The mark preserves the
selected green-and-warm-white concept: two heads, two interlocking paths, a
hidden `A`, and a protected doorway at the centre.

The identity must feel Pakistani and familiar without relying on flags,
landmarks, tools, roofs, map pins, or decorative cultural clichés.

## Positioning

**Category:** local-services marketplace.

**Audience promise:** Find verified local help, compare private offers, choose
for yourself, and pay cash after the work.

**Provider promise:** Receive relevant nearby opportunities, choose the work
that fits, and offer a fair price without a hidden acceptance score.

**Primary brand line:** Kaam ka sahi match.

**English support line:** The right local help, chosen by you.

Never claim provider counts, arrival times, ratings, nationwide supply, or
guaranteed outcomes unless live first-party data supports the statement.

## Personality

- Calm, capable, and neighbourly.
- Clear before clever.
- Safety-led, but never fear-led.
- Local without slang that excludes people.
- Fair to customers and providers.
- Modern technology expressed through outcomes, not AI hype.

## Voice

Use short plain-English or natural Roman Urdu sentences. Prefer verbs and
specific outcomes.

Use:

- “Post what needs fixing.”
- “Compare private offers.”
- “Exact address private until you choose.”
- “Kaam ka sahi match.”

Avoid:

- “Revolutionary,” “world-class,” “number one,” or “guaranteed.”
- Fake urgency, fake activity, or unverified social proof.
- Mixing English and Roman Urdu mid-sentence unless the result sounds natural.
- Calling credits cash, money, deposits, or earnings before work is completed.

## Core colours

- **Apna Green** `#075B55`: primary identity and actions on light surfaces.
- **Deep Green** `#082F2C`: app icon, dark surfaces, and premium contrast.
- **Warm White** `#F7F4EC`: selected logo partner and warm page background.
- **Ink** `#13211E`: primary text.
- **Quiet Green** `#5F716C`: secondary text.
- **Mint Surface** `#E6F3EF`: calm supporting surfaces.
- **Line** `#DCE6E2`: dividers and boundaries.
- **Icon Teal** `#0B6A62`: a launcher-only secondary green that keeps the
  selected two-ribbon mark legible on Deep Green. It is not a UI action colour.

Success, warning, danger, information, ratings, and finance colours remain
semantic tokens; they are not additional brand colours. Never communicate a
state through colour alone.

## Typography

Use **Manrope** for the public website, operations console, campaigns, and
Latin/Roman Urdu brand lockups:

- Display/wordmark basis: 800.
- Headings: 700.
- Controls: 600.
- Body: 400–500.

The native customer/provider application intentionally keeps the iOS/Android
system font for familiar platform rendering, dynamic type, startup speed, and
low-memory devices. The standalone wordmark and horizontal lockups are supplied
as outlined Manrope ExtraBold vector masters; product UI may still use a live
heavy system-font lockup where dynamic type or localization requires it. Retain
the SIL Open Font License anywhere Manrope is distributed.

## Logo usage

Source assets live in `assets/brand`.

- `apnatask-mark.svg`: selected green-and-warm-white master for dark or
  photographic surfaces.
- `apnatask-mark-green.svg`: one-colour mark for light surfaces.
- `apnatask-mark-white.svg`: reversed mark for dark surfaces.
- `apnatask-app-icon.svg`: full-bleed launcher icon source.
- `apnatask-adaptive-foreground.svg`: Android adaptive foreground source.
- `apnatask-monochrome.svg`: themed/notification source.
- `apnatask-favicon.svg`: simplified micro-mark.
- `apnatask-wordmark-outlined.svg`: font-independent standalone wordmark.
- `apnatask-lockup-horizontal-outlined.svg`: mark and wordmark for light surfaces.
- `apnatask-lockup-horizontal-reversed-outlined.svg`: reversed lockup for dark surfaces.

The Vercel-ready public-site package also contains 180, 192, and 512 px web
icons, regular and maskable PWA exports, and a 1200×630 social-sharing card.

Rules:

- Keep clear space equal to one head diameter around the mark.
- Use the full mark at 24 px or larger.
- Use the simplified favicon/micro-mark below 24 px.
- Do not redraw, rotate, stretch, outline, recolour individual pieces, or put
  the wordmark inside an app icon.
- Do not add gradients, bevels, shadows, glows, or animation inside the mark.
- On a light surface use the one-colour green mark; the warm-white ribbon is
  reserved for a contrasting dark surface.

## Motion

When motion is appropriate, the two paths may arrive from opposite sides and
settle into the completed `A`. Duration: 240–360 ms. The doorway appears only
after the paths meet. Provide a no-motion state and never loop the animation.

## Photography and illustration

Show real work and context: hands diagnosing a problem, a provider preparing
tools, a customer checking completed work, or a map showing privacy-safe
locality. Represent varied Pakistani homes, ages, genders, and service roles.

Avoid staged handshakes, hard-hat stock-photo teams, exposed CNIC/address data,
unsafe work practices, and imagery that implies a provider is an ApnaTask
employee.

## Product application

- Customer and provider modes share one brand; mode is communicated through
  labels and navigation, not different logos.
- The admin console uses the one-colour white mark with “Operations.”
- Exact-location privacy, KYC review, private offers, cash-after-work, and
  completion confirmation are the preferred trust proof points.
- English and Roman Urdu are the only active interface languages.

## Required release checks

- Inspect at 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 180, 512, and 1024 px.
- Test light, dark, grayscale, high-contrast, Android themed icons, and common
  adaptive masks.
- Verify adjacent text and controls meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
- Confirm all raster exports are sRGB and that launcher icons are opaque.
- Run a Pakistan and WIPO word/device-mark search before trademark filing.
