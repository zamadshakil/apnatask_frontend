# Preferred mark candidate

This is a clean vector interpretation of the newly selected raster reference. It intentionally keeps the reference's broad, asymmetric two-person construction while removing the generated edge noise and broken overlap around the doorway.

## Geometry

- Canvas: `256 × 256`, with visible artwork from approximately `x=27–229` and `y=23–238` once round caps are included.
- Heads: equal `21`-unit circles at `(86, 44)` and `(171, 44)`. Their `85`-unit separation preserves the reference's unequal relationship to the central roof rather than forcing visual symmetry.
- Ribbon width: `36` units for the green figure and `40` units for the cream figure. The slight optical correction reproduces the reference's broader foreground sweep while keeping the two figures equal in perceived weight.
- Rear/green shoulder: a centreline begins at `(64, 209)`, rises through a shoulder centred near `(114, 87)`, and disappears beneath the cream roof at `(147, 91)`.
- Front/cream figure: one continuous centreline begins at `(48, 207)`, rises through the main roof apex around `(145, 88)`, then descends to `(188, 207)`.
- Woven overlap: the green right arm returns as a separate centreline at `(150, 114)` and descends toward `(209, 209)`. The hidden break is entirely covered by the cream roof. This deliberately changes the stacking order once—cream crosses above the green left shoulder, then green crosses above the cream right leg—matching the reference without a visible seam or clipping artifact.
- Doorway: spans `x=103–153`, reaches `y=144` at its soft gable, and lands on the shared `y=222` baseline. It is a separate filled silhouette so the symbol remains recognizable at small sizes.
- Curves: only cubic Béziers and round caps/joins are used. There are no masks, filters, raster images, strokes with miter spikes, or self-intersecting polygons.

## Color

- Full color: a restrained green gradient (`#087068 → #004C47`) and warm-white gradient (`#FFFDF8 → #F1ECE2`) reproduce the depth in the reference without texture or effects.
- Green-only: solid brand green `#075B55`, suitable for print, embossing, and single-ink use.
- Reversed: warm white `#FFFDF8`, intended for deep-green or photographic backgrounds.

All three SVGs have transparent canvases and accessible title/description metadata.
